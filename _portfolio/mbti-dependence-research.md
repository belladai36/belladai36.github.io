As initiator and team lead for a five-person China Thinks Big project, I studied why teenagers increasingly use MBTI personality labels to interpret identity, relationships, and behavior.

The project combined literature review, survey research, psychometric critique, science communication, and program evaluation. An initial survey of **118 respondents** examined MBTI awareness and reliance. We also reviewed limitations involving test-retest reliability, validity, binary personality categories, translation, administration, and result interpretation.

### Intervention

Our team designed a short interactive educational video that:

- assessed viewers' level of reliance through scenario-based questions,
- explained limitations of personality tests,
- and provided different reflection and prevention strategies based on viewers' responses.

The video received more than **4,000 views across Bilibili, Weibo, and QQ**, along with comments and audience reflections. A follow-up survey collected **297 responses** to evaluate changes in viewers' understanding and willingness to reduce reliance on MBTI labels.

The project qualified for the **2023 China Thinks Big Global Final**.
